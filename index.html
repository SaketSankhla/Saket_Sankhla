About

I am an ECE undergraduate at GECA Ajmer with a focus on digital VLSI, RTL design, and embedded systems. My approach is driven by a project-first implementation philosophy — building practical systems from the ground up.

I specialize in designing synthesizable Verilog RTL blocks, integrating AMBA APB/AHB bus protocols, and programming low-latency control firmware on microcontrollers.

My primary roadmap is targeted towards GATE 2027 with a target score of 720–750 to pursue an M.Tech in VLSI at IIT Bombay. Currently, I am actively building my expertise through hands-on design and verification projects, and researching hardware architecture.
